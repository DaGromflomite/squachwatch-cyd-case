## SquachWatch portable case: v20

**What's new:** bigger SQUACHWATCH badges, now rounded-rectangle nameplates with clean meshes (so screw holes and pockets stay open when sliced in Bambu Studio). The charging-port hole is now big enough for the plug body of a normal USB-C cable. On the **Horizontal** case the lettering moves to the back, above Squachy, and the bezel is plain. On the **Vertical** case the lettering sits on the bezel under the screen, in two big lines.

**Downloads**
- `SquachWatch_Portable_Build_Guide_v11.pdf`: the full build guide (parts, flashing, wiring, case assembly)
- `squachwatch_case_v20.3mf`: Bambu Studio project with 3 plates (1 Horizontal, 2 Plain, 3 Vertical), colors set
- `SquachWatch_STL_v20.zip`: every part as an STL, for any slicer

**Hardware:** 4 × M4 × 6 × 6 mm heat-set inserts, 4 × M4 × 8 mm button-head screws, CA glue for the badges.
